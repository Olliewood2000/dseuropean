## Section 8. FAQ

```
Block: FAQ
Background: surface
Padding: standard
Container: narrow
```

**Title:** Common questions

**Can we set up a regular weekly route?**
> Yes, and that is a large part of what we do. Once the route, the timings and the site details are agreed, it runs to that pattern without needing to be rebooked each time.

**Do you offer same day collection and delivery?**
> Yes. Same day is available for day to day movements and is one of the main reasons businesses keep us on the account, because it covers the week where something goes wrong.

**What size loads do you cover?**
> Anything from a single item in a Luton up to full loads on 18 tonne curtain or box vehicles. If the volume changes between runs we use the vehicle that suits that week rather than the one on the contract.

**What happens if our volumes change month to month?**
> That is normal and does not need renegotiating every time. Seasonal businesses and project driven volumes are common among our customers and the schedule flexes with them.

**Will we be dealing with the same people each time?**
> Yes. We are a team of twenty one, so the person who answers the phone knows the account rather than reading it off a screen for the first time.

**Can you deliver to our customers on our behalf?**
> Yes, and for several of our clients we already do, including dealing directly with their customers at the delivery point. If that is how you want it handled, say so at the start and we will work to your standards rather than ours.

## Claims requiring client sign off

1. Dedicated rather than consolidated as a standard, since any groupage work would contradict it
2. Pricing regular work on the route rather than per job
3. Setting up a new route within roughly two weeks
4. Whether accounts get a named contact formally, or whether that is simply what happens at their size
5. Multi drop routes, which are not mentioned anywhere in the discovery answers

Section 5 is built directly on the client's own written statement about being treated as an integral part of their customers' businesses. It is the strongest material in the whole discovery pack, which is why it gets a full band. Confirm they are comfortable with it stated publicly, since it describes their relationships rather than their capability.
