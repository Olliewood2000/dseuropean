## 2. What DS European actually does

DS European moves high value and sensitive goods for businesses, then installs or sets them up at the other end. The transport is only half the job. The part that makes them different is what happens on site: fitters, installers, project supervision, and in many cases managing the whole job on the client's behalf.

For a lot of their customers they operate as an extension of the client's own team. They will liaise directly with the client's customers, manage the project on site, and represent the brand. That is the single most important thing to understand about this business and it should shape the positioning.

The storage and fulfilment side completes the offer. It means a job can be collected, stored, consolidated, delivered, and installed without handing off to a third party.

**One line summary:** a bespoke logistics and installation company that transports, stores, and installs high value goods across the UK, Europe, and worldwide, with an on site team that handles the job end to end.

## 3. Service lines

### Customer facing categories (for the website)

1. **Furniture**: bespoke, luxury, and designer pieces handled with care from collection to installation.
2. **Retail displays**: pop-up units, store fixtures, and brand activation materials delivered securely and on schedule.
3. **Exhibitions**: show stands, event builds, and promotional setups transported directly to venue.
4. **Equipment**: commercial, specialist, and technical equipment moved safely with professional handling.
5. **Business deliveries**: scheduled or repeat routes for businesses requiring a dependable transport partner.
6. **Private items**: one-off personal deliveries for items that cannot be entrusted to standard couriers.

### Services the client named that these six categories do not yet cover

- **Office relocations and installations**: named as a core service, currently missing from the six.
- **Event transportation and on site handling**: goes beyond delivery to venue.
- **Event management**: the client lists this as a service they deliver.
- **Interior design project installation**: their flagship work, delivered globally.
- **White glove logistics**: the handling standard across everything.
- **Same day**: available for day to day work.
- **Storage and fulfilment**: described by the client as their competitive edge.

**Action:** the six categories are cargo types. The client's answers are service types. The site needs both, or the six need widening so office moves, on site installation, and storage are visible.

## 7. Brand and tone

**Mission statement (client's own words):** DS European Ltd are a safe, reliable, efficient logistics and installation company that offers bespoke solutions with vast experience and exceptional capability.

**Want to be known for:** reliability, honesty, trustworthiness.

### Visual identity (from DS European Brand Guidelines 2025)

| | |
|---|---|
| Vivid blue | #0156C7 |
| Dark blue | #000056 |
| White | #FFFFFF |
| Typeface | Montserrat, Regular and Bold |
| Logo | Long and short formats, each with a white background and dark background variant |

Shape motif: angled slashes and rounded-corner parallelograms, drawn from the logo. Use as section dividers, image clipping, and card corners.

Extensions needed for web, which the guidelines do not cover:
- A cool off-white section tint, around #F4F6FA, for alternating bands
- A light border grey
- A softened dark blue for body copy
- Semantic colours for form validation

Rules for the site:
- Vivid blue for links, buttons, accents and stat figures only. Never for body copy.
- Dark blue for headings and body text.
- Page rhythm comes from navy bands against white, not from a third colour.

**Brand statement in the guidelines:** specialist logistics across the UK and Europe, moving high value and bespoke items with precision, reliability and care, delivering peace of mind rather than just goods.

Note that this describes a transport company. It omits installation, on site teams, storage and fulfilment, and the worldwide work. Site copy should go further than the deck.

**Photography in the guidelines is decorative, not a rule.** The sunset fleet, motorway blur and hi-vis stock shots should not carry into the site. Branded vans, polos and real job photography only.

**Tone:** professional and understated. This is a B2B audience buying peace of mind for expensive, fragile, or irreplaceable goods. Confidence without hype. No courier language, no price led messaging, no volume claims.

**Messaging pillars**
1. End to end: collected, stored, delivered, installed, by one team.
2. On site capability: fitters and supervisors, not just drivers.
3. Bespoke: every job planned around the client, not slotted into a route.
4. An extension of your team: they represent the client in front of the client's own customers.
5. Reach: UK, Europe, and worldwide, including live international installation work.

## 9. Claims we can make, and claims we cannot

**Safe to use**
- Over 60 years combined industry experience
- UK, Europe, and worldwide delivery
- 24/7, 365 availability
- Same day available
- In house installation and fitting team
- Storage and fulfilment
- International interior design installations (Lake Como, Girona, France, Texas)
- Extensive global partnerships

**Do not use until evidenced**
- Any insurance or goods in transit figure
- Any accreditation logo or membership
- Named clients or testimonials
- Number of jobs, vehicles, or years trading
- "Fully insured" as a bare claim without the cover level confirmed
- Anything implying they are the largest, cheapest, or fastest

## 10. Open questions to close with the client

**Blocking the build**
1. Project photos and case studies. Promised but not yet supplied. The site is weak without them, particularly for the Lake Como, Girona, and Texas jobs.
2. Canonical domain. Confirm .com or .co.uk as primary, and redirect the other.
3. Primary phone number for the site. Three are currently in circulation. Pick one, ideally the one that will later carry call tracking.
4. Enquiry inbox. Both gary@ and accounts@ exist. A sales@ or info@ alias is still worth setting up so new business does not land in accounts.
5. Logo files in SVG, both formats and both background variants.

**Needed for trust content**
6. Goods in transit and liability cover levels.
7. Operator licence, and whether they hold their own international licence or work through partners.
8. Accreditations and memberships: FORS, BAR, ISO, trade bodies.
9. Storage location, capacity, and whether it is secure, insured, or bonded.
10. Post Brexit EU setup: customs handling, CMR, carnets, who handles paperwork.

**Needed for content depth**
11. Two or three anonymised job stories with the problem, the solution, and the outcome.
12. Any testimonials from repeat clients.
13. Company registration and VAT numbers for the footer.
14. Existing social profiles and any Google Business Profile.
15. Clarification on event management: is this full event management or on site logistics management, because the wording changes what they are selling.
